# Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, cut releases and enforce the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). How someone becomes a maintainer is in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## How to Reach the Maintainers

- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/ramprasadgk9/ops-signals/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [Issues](https://github.com/ramprasadgk9/ops-signals/issues).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Code of Conduct reports:** [opssignals.conduct@gmail.com](mailto:opssignals.conduct@gmail.com),
  which reaches every maintainer.
