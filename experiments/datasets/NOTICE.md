# Third-party data

`rds_cpu_utilization_cc0c53.csv` is `data/realAWSCloudwatch/rds_cpu_utilization_cc0c53.csv` from the
[Numenta Anomaly Benchmark (NAB)](https://github.com/numenta/NAB), unchanged, at commit
`ea702d75cc2258d9d7dd35ca8e5e2539d71f3140`. `rds_cpu_utilization_cc0c53_router.csv` is the same
series reshaped for the analyzer router experiment. The plots and report under
`experiments/ruptures/` are made from it.
